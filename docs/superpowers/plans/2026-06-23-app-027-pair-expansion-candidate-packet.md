## Selection Basis

**Source:** `docs/pair-expansion-targets.md`, generated by `npm run audit:pair-targets`.

**Selected groups:**

| # | Category | Group ID | Contrast | Missing early pairs (T1–T3) | Selection reason |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 日本語 (Japanese) | bV | b/v | 6 | /v/ is absent in Japanese phonology; flagship contrast for Japanese learners |
| 2 | 中文 (Mandarin) | vW | v/w | 6 | /v/ is absent in standard Mandarin; flagship contrast for Mandarin learners |
| 3 | Español (Spanish) | iVsI | iː/ɪ | 4 | Major Spanish–English vowel contrast; partially filled (2 pairs at T1 and T2) |
| 4 | العربية (Arabic) | pB | p/b | 6 | /p/ is absent in Arabic phonology; highest-priority Arabic group |
| 5 | فارسی (Farsi) | wV | w/v | 6 | w/v confusion common in Farsi learners; highest-priority Farsi group |

**Why these groups, not the mathematical top-5:**

The priority backlog sorts alphabetically after score, placing Bahasa Indonesia groups
at ranks 1–5 and CJK/Arabic-named categories lower. All 5 selected groups score 28 or 19
on the priority scale and carry HIGH severity at tiers 1–3. They were selected because:
- They represent flagship L1 contrasts for large learner populations
- They cover 4 distinct contrast types (b/v, v/w, iː/ɪ, p/b) for batch variety
- The task spec designates them as the default batch scope when all are present and high-priority

No substitution from the default scope was needed.

## Human Review Checklist

For each candidate pair, a human reviewer should confirm:

- [ ] **IPA accuracy** — verify IPA against a reliable reference (e.g., Merriam-Webster,
  Cambridge) for General American pronunciation
- [ ] **Target contrast** — confirm the ONLY phonemic difference between word1 and word2 is
  the target contrast phoneme pair (or note if it is near-minimal)
- [ ] **Word familiarity** — confirm both words are appropriate for the intended tier and
  learner population
- [ ] **TTS reliability** — synthesize both words with the app TTS voice and confirm the
  target contrast is perceptually clear in the audio output
- [ ] **Dialect robustness** — confirm the contrast holds in General American; note any
  regional variations that may erode it
- [ ] **Duplicate/reversed-duplicate check** — confirm the pair does not already exist in
  the same category × group in either word order
- [ ] **Tier assignment** — confirm the tier assignment reflects actual learner difficulty,
  not just vocabulary frequency
- [ ] **Approve or reject** — mark each candidate as approved, rejected, or needing further
  review before the next implementation PR
