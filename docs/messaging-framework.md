# Soundwise Messaging Framework

## Purpose

This document is the canonical messaging and positioning reference for Soundwise. Use it to maintain consistency across App Store copy, community replies, landing page text, SEO content, and any future marketing materials.

It is intentionally concise. Read it before writing anything public-facing.

## Last Updated

2026-05-07

---

## Positioning Statement

Soundwise helps English learners train their listening perception — the ability to reliably hear the difference between similar-sounding English sounds — through focused minimal-pair practice.

---

## One-Sentence Elevator Pitch

> Soundwise is an ear-training app for English learners that helps you hear the difference between words that sound almost the same.

Use this or a close variation when introducing the product in a single sentence. Do not add claims beyond what is stated here.

---

## Core Messaging Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Ear first** | Listening perception is the foundation. You need to hear a distinction reliably before you can produce it consistently. |
| **Focused practice** | Minimal-pair drills isolate the exact sounds that cause confusion. This is deliberate, specific work. |
| **Real English words** | All practice uses real English word pairs, not invented nonsense words. |
| **Adaptive progression** | The app adjusts difficulty as you improve — faster playback, harder word contexts. |
| **Immediate feedback** | After each response, you see the correct word, its IPA, and the contrast phoneme highlighted. |
| **14 L1 backgrounds** | Word pairs are curated for learners from 14 different first-language backgrounds. |

## Claim Boundaries

Soundwise may claim:

- It uses minimal-pair forced-choice identification, an established technique in L2 phoneme perception research.
- Immediate feedback and adaptive progression are implemented features.
- Voice variation (randomized TTS from the device's installed English voices) exposes learners to different acoustic profiles.
- Progress is tracked per word pair.
- It supports learners from 14 first-language backgrounds with tailored word-pair sets.

Soundwise must not claim:

- Clinical or scientific validation of Soundwise specifically (no trials have been conducted).
- Guaranteed improvement in pronunciation production.
- Accent elimination or neutralization.
- Fluency outcomes.
- Results within any fixed time period.

If asked about the research basis, the accurate framing is: minimal-pair perceptual training is a well-studied technique in second-language acquisition research. Soundwise applies that technique in an accessible mobile format.

## CTA Hierarchy

Use CTAs in this order of preference:

1. **Educational first** — answer the question, teach something useful.
2. **Soft mention** — "There's a free app called Soundwise that drills exactly this."
3. **Direct link** — only when someone has asked for a resource or the context clearly warrants it.

Never lead with a CTA. Never post a link without substantive content.

## Audience Segments

| Segment | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ESL learner (self-directed) | Studying English independently; likely intermediate; frustrated with specific sounds. |
| IELTS/test-prep learner | Preparing for a listening or speaking exam; motivated but time-constrained. |
| ESL teacher | Looking for supplemental resources for students; values credibility and practicality. |
| Native-language community member | Encounters the product through Reddit or YouTube; not actively searching. |

---

## Audience-Specific Framing

**ESL learner (self-directed)**
Lead with the specific problem they mentioned. Acknowledge the difficulty is real and common. Offer the practice mechanism. Mention the app if it fits.

**IELTS / test-prep learner**
Frame listening perception as a prerequisite for the listening section. Emphasize that recognizing words accurately in fast speech is a trainable skill. Offer the app as a drill tool.

**ESL teacher**
Be direct about what the app does and does not do. Teachers appreciate precision. It is a supplemental ear-training tool, not a full curriculum. Mention that it is free, self-paced, and focused.

**Community observer (non-active learner)**
Do not pitch. They have not asked. If they engage, respond to the engagement.

## App Store Messaging Guidance

- Lead with the core value proposition in the first two lines of the description (visible before truncation).
- Use plain English in the title and subtitle — avoid jargon.
- Describe the learning mechanism clearly: listen, identify, get feedback.
- Do not make unverifiable claims about outcomes.
- Use keywords that match learner search behavior: "minimal pairs," "ear training," "English listening," "pronunciation practice."
- Screenshots should demonstrate the practice loop, feedback, and language-selection features.

---

## Reddit / Community Messaging Guidance

- Reply as a knowledgeable peer, not as a promoter.
- Answer the question being asked before anything else.
- Mention Soundwise only when the thread is specifically about ear training, minimal pairs, or a confusion the app directly addresses.
- Do not post the same reply template across multiple threads. Vary phrasing naturally.
- If someone has already received good advice, add to it rather than redirecting to the product.
- Do not post the App Store link in every reply. Use it contextually, in threads where someone has asked for a resource.
- See `docs/response-template-library.md` for specific reply examples.

---

## SEO Messaging Guidance

- Target learner pain queries, not product-first queries.
- Write content that genuinely answers "why do sheep and ship sound the same to me?" before mentioning the app.
- The app should appear as a natural next step, not as the primary subject of every page.
- Avoid keyword stuffing. Write for the learner first.
- See `docs/seo-keyword-map.md` for keyword clusters and content targets.

## What Soundwise Is

- A listening perception trainer for English learners.
- A minimal-pair identification app.
- A focused drill tool that adjusts difficulty as you improve.
- A supplement to broader English study.
- A free iOS app available on the App Store.

---

## What Soundwise Is Not

- A full English curriculum.
- A pronunciation production coach.
- A grammar or vocabulary tool.
- A speech therapy application.
- A guaranteed path to any accent or fluency level.
- A substitute for real conversation practice.

## Examples of Good Messaging

> "The /r/–/l/ contrast is notoriously hard for Japanese and Mandarin learners because both sounds map to a single category in those languages. Drilling minimal pairs — hearing 'rake' vs. 'lake' repeatedly with feedback — is one of the best-studied ways to train your ear to pick up the difference."

> "Soundwise is a free ear-training app that drills this kind of listening distinction. It's not a magic fix, but the repetition is real practice."

> "Struggling with ship vs. sheep is extremely common. Your brain is filtering English sounds through your L1 — it takes deliberate listening practice to reset that filter."

---

## Examples of Bad Messaging

> ❌ "Soundwise will fix your accent and make you sound like a native speaker in just 10 minutes a day."

> ❌ "Clinically proven ear training technology."

> ❌ "Don't miss out — download now before the free period ends."

> ❌ "I made an app that solves this! [link] [link] [link]"

> ❌ "As a language expert, I can tell you that Soundwise is the most scientifically advanced pronunciation tool available."
